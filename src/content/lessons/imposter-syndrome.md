# The thoughts in your mind come first

The first and most important leason is dealing with your own mind. That starts with imposter syndrome. Being self taught this sense of doubt is always going to be something you have to deal with and others may capitalize on to belittle you. Getting your self-esteem up and tackling this issue head on is your first step.
